# Story 7: Teach it what matters — priority signals

**Status:** Approved
**Created:** 2026-07-24
**Type:** Feature
**Epic:** `second-brain` (#7) · PRD §5.6, §6, §7.2, §7.6

## Background
The owner's relative-value judgment — *this* matters more than *that*, today — is the scarcest input in the delegation loop, and today it evaporates the moment it's exercised: story 6's proposals record what was chosen, but not the owner's underlying sense of what matters. Story 7 captures that judgment as durable data. The owner answers quick pairwise questions — *"solve one today: which?"* — choosing one goal over the other, optionally with a one-line reason. Each choice becomes a **Priority Signal**: a dated, attributed, append-only fact tagged with the **framing** that produced it (PRD §5.6, §6).

The framing tag is the load-bearing product idea. The operator's stated epistemology — *99 wrong framings, find the right one by iterating* — means the pairwise question itself is expected to be replaced as better framings are found (a replacement is proposed with evidence and ratified by the owner — PRD §7.6; the constitution binds it). Signals therefore record *how they were elicited*, so that when the framing changes, the accumulated history stays interpretable rather than becoming silently incommensurable. **V1 ships pairwise only, records only:** no score aggregation, no ranking display, no framing-management surface. The signals are data for the future — the raw material a later selection engine may consult and a proposal's why-now *may* cite in plain words — **not a feature surface now** (queue note). Nothing ever launches or gets decided from a signal (PRD §5.6, §7.1).

This is the last producer story before the book's close pair: story 8's export names **signals** among the owner-authored content it must carry, so story 7 completes the PRD §6 entity set's live producers ahead of it (Category instances remain the epic's recorded coverage gap).

Affected: **The Delegating Owner** (journeys 3/6 — teaching the system their sense of value in seconds, without ceremony). The Fresh-Context Session benefits downstream (richer rationale available to cite) but the owner journey is the justification (traceability guard).

## User-facing description
As the owner, I want to answer quick either-or questions about my goals — "solve one today: which?" — choosing one over the other with an optional one-line reason, so that my sense of what matters accumulates as dated, attributed facts on the goals themselves — visible where I look, citable by future proposals in plain words, and never silently re-interpreted when the question that elicited them changes — while nothing is ever launched or decided from them without me.

## Acceptance criteria
Testable from the outside (input → observable behavior). Owner-facing strings follow `product-team/guides/second-brain-style-guide.md` (register, banned jargon, comparisons-not-decimals); the goal-detail record spine follows `product-team/guides/second-brain-design-guide.md`. **Both guides have a known gap for signals** (no signal record-type word, no signal component, no capture confirmation) — the exact owner-facing wording is authored to register and **ratified at a gate**, per the story-6 approve-string precedent (see Open questions #1/#4). Writes ride the epic's settled discipline: gated, validated before any write, serialized, local-only, append-only by construction.

- [ ] **AC1 — The owner can record a pairwise choice between two goals, with an optional one-line reason.** Given a brain holding at least two goals, when the owner answers the pairwise question — *"solve one today: which?"* (PRD §5.6's canonical elicitation) — by choosing one goal over another, with or without a one-line reason, then a Priority Signal is recorded capturing the **preferred goal**, the **passed-over goal**, and the **reason when given**. A choice naming an unknown or ambiguous goal is **refused** with a plain-language explanation and records nothing; a choice of a goal **over itself** is refused and records nothing. *(Testable: a valid choice reads back as one signal with prefers/over/optional reason; an unknown-goal choice and a self-choice are each refused with nothing written.)*

- [ ] **AC2 — Every signal is dated, attributed, and framing-tagged.** Given any recorded signal, it carries **who judged** (the owner, as the brain knows them), **when** (the judged-on date), and **the framing that produced it** (the framing tag) — all three present on every signal, not optional (PRD §6). *(Testable: read back any recorded signal → judged-by, judged-on, and framing tag are present and non-empty.)*

- [ ] **AC3 — Signals are append-only and visible on both goals they touch.** Given a recorded signal preferring goal A over goal B, then an entry for it appears on **both** A's and B's record spines (the goal detail's append-only record section), each side worded in plain comparative language readable from that goal's perspective (this goal was chosen over the other / the other was chosen over this one — exact wording per Open questions #1). Record entries expose **no edit or delete affordance**; a recorded signal is never rewritten or removed — a correction or a changed mind is a **new** signal (PRD §7.2). *(Testable: after recording, A's goal detail shows the signal entry and B's goal detail shows the signal entry, each with its date and side-appropriate wording; no edit/delete affordance exists on them; recording a second, opposite signal leaves the first intact on both spines.)*

- [ ] **AC4 — Signals never launch or decide anything; proposals may cite them in plain words.** Given any number of recorded signals, **no** code path launches a session, schedules work, produces a proposal, or decides one *from* a signal — recording a signal changes nothing in the world except the record itself, and the story-6 proposal loop's behavior is unchanged (its rationale remains caller-supplied; it does not consume signals). A proposal's why-now **may** cite recorded signals **in plain words** (the conversational lane may read signals and mention them — e.g. "you've chosen this over X twice this week"); the citation is words in a why-now, never a mechanism. *(Testable: recording signals triggers no proposal, no decision, and no launch side effect; the proposal-making path's inputs are unchanged; a why-now containing plain-word signal citations renders like any why-now, with no numeric score.)*

- [ ] **AC5 — A replaced framing leaves earlier signals interpretable.** Given recorded signals, each signal's framing identification is carried **by the signal itself** and read back **from** it — never derived at read time from a global "current framing" — so replacing the elicitation framing later cannot silently re-label history: a signal recorded under an earlier framing still identifies how it was produced. *(Testable: a signal's read-back includes its own framing tag; signals carrying distinct framing tags coexist, each keeping its own; no read path substitutes a signal's stored framing with a live/global value.)*

- [ ] **AC6 — Copy discipline, comparisons-not-decimals, the append-only concept, and no regression.** Every owner-facing string this story adds — the spine wording for both sides, the pairwise prompt where surfaced, any capture confirmation — passes the **banned-jargon scan** (*element, kind, schema, event, pubkey, superset, concept header, persona, acceptance criteria, lease, payload, endpoint*), carries **no exclamation marks**, and renders relative value in **words only**: no numeric score, weight, percentage, tally, gauge, or ranking number appears anywhere owner-facing (style guide "comparisons, not decimals"; design principle 2 — this includes not surfacing signal *counts* as numbers on goal surfaces). The Priority Signal concept is **new, append-only, runtime-created, never firmware-seeded**, with the assistant identity **resolved at runtime, never hardcoded** (PRD §7.8); a fresh instance self-provisions on first use and an empty brain still offers its one obvious action (second-operator guard §5.9). The brain surfaces stories 1–6 shipped continue to pass their suites, amended only where this story legitimately extends them (the goal-detail record spine now also carries signal entries).

## Out of scope
- **Score aggregation, rankings, tallies, or any numeric rendering of signals.** No "A beats B 3–1," no leaderboard, no sort-by-signals anywhere. The signals are data for the future, not a feature surface now (queue note; PRD §8.3 defers the selection engine).
- **A framing registry, framing-swap mechanism, or framing-management surface.** §7.6's replacement flow (proposed with evidence, ratified by the owner) is a later phase; v1 records the tag on each signal, nothing more.
- **A new owner-facing view or capture form.** V1's screen inventory is fixed at three views (design guide, binding; PRD §5.8) — signal capture rides the conversational lane (the design guide's capture pattern: capture happens in conversation, not in this UI), and visibility rides the **existing** Goal detail spine. No signals browser, no capture modal.
- **Wiring signals into the proposer.** Story 6's proposal-making path does not consume signals and this story does not change that; AC4's "may cite" is a conversational affordance, not plumbing. A brain-side selection engine that weighs signals is Phase 2/3 (PRD §8.3).
- **Anything launching or auto-deciding from signals** — §5.6/§7.1; recorded, never acted on autonomously, in v1.
- **Editing, deleting, or retracting a signal.** Append-only; corrections are new facts (§7.2).
- **Bulk elicitation, tournaments, or comparisons of more than two goals at once.** Pairwise only in v1.
- **Signal coverage in the hygiene check** — follows the story-6 precedent (Proposal is not hygiene-covered either; a later natural addition).

## Open questions
Resolved from the queue + PRD + guides + the story-7 handoff:
- Dependencies: **story 1** (goals exist) is Done and in production; story 6 (pairs naturally, not required) is also Done. Satisfied.
- Signals merge into the existing goal-detail record spine; the RecordEntry rendering already accepts any type word (story-6 recon), so the surface question is wording, not machinery.

Deferred to a gate (intent fixed; wording/mechanism proposed by the Architect, ratified by the operator — the story-6 d16 precedent):
1. **THE story-spine question — the two-goal spine representation and its wording.** The design guide's record vocabulary (`proposed / approved / skipped / worked / noted`) has **no signal word**, and a signal is the first record that touches **two** goals. The intent is fixed: the signal appears on **both** spines, in plain comparative words readable from each goal's side, with no numbers. The record-type word (one word for both sides? a side-specific pair?) and the exact spine strings are the Architect's to propose and the operator's to **ratify at a gate** before implementation copy is written.
2. **Must the compared goals hold any particular standing?** Default carried in AC1: **no** — any two **distinct, real** goals may be compared; viability is *not* required (unlike a proposal's nominee — the owner is teaching relative value, not certifying execution-readiness; a parent or captured goal can legitimately matter more than another goal). Confirm at the gate.
3. **Who supplies the framing tag in v1?** Default: the recording lane stamps the canonical v1 framing (the pairwise *"solve one today: which?"* elicitation) when none is stated, and the tag's form (a short slug? the question text?) is the Architect's. Every signal must carry the framing that actually produced it (AC2/AC5); there is no framing-management surface (out of scope). Confirm at the gate.
4. **The capture confirmation sentence (style-guide gap).** The style guide pins confirmations for capture, skip, and restore — nothing for a recorded signal. If the conversational capture surfaces an in-view confirmation, its canonical one-sentence form must be authored to register and **ratified at a gate**, not invented at implementation (story-6 Open-Q4 precedent). The ratified strings should be back-filled into the style guide at book-close alongside the approve string (ADR 0006 d16's return-edge note).
5. **Attribution's shape.** V1 has one judge — the owner. "Judged-by" must be recorded so a second operator's instance (and any future multi-judge world) stays interpretable (§5.9); its concrete form is the Architect's.
