### Blocking
1. **`BIBLE.md:1076`** (the same claim at **`BIBLE.md:497`**, **`src/api/openapi.yaml:358`**, **`src/api/index.js:560-561`** and **`src/api/assistant/identificationTaggings.js:5`**) — the sentence this story adds to § Assistant Keys, *"An assistant's key signs two more things on the person's request, and nothing else outside the generic signer's owner-only path"*, and its four echoes (*"The only thing besides its profile an assistant's key signs on request"*; *"The one route through which an Assistant's key signs something other than its profile"*), are false. Verified with `grep -rn 'getAssistantKeys(\|getOwnerAssistantKeys(\|finalizeEvent(' src`: `POST /api/dlist-curation/header` (`src/api/dlist-curation/index.js:246-247`, signed through `:232`) and `POST /api/dlist-curation/update` (`src/api/dlist-curation/update.js:287`, `:295`) sign kind-39998 curation headers and updates with `getAssistantKeys(sessionPubkey)` — the signed-in person's own Assistant, on the person's request, through routes of their own; the trusted-list routes (`src/api/trustedList/index.js:46`, `:60-70`, behind `requireAuth` at `:186`) sign kinds 30392–30395 and the 30000/39089 exports with the TA on request; the normalize routes (`src/api/normalize/helpers.js:30-45`, `src/api/normalize/index.js:91-107`) sign with the TA on request. The kind-scoped sentence this one imitates (assistant-profile ADR 0005's "No other path signs an assistant kind 0") is true; this one is unscoped and is not. ADR 0003 sub-decision 11 (`0003-…md:211`) prescribed the wording, so the defect is the ADR's, carried into the BIBLE (the universal, fork-agnostic document), the API contract and two code comments — the very paragraph that states the signing boundary. No test pins the sentence (checked with `grep -rn` over `test` and `tests`). **Asked change:** reword the five spots to a true, scoped statement — for example: *its two identification taggings of the person, through their own narrow, session-bound route (the second such route after `publish-profile`); the generic signer `/api/strfry/publish` stays owner-only, TA-only and unchanged; the other server paths that sign with assistant keys (trusted lists, curated DLists, normalization, NIP-85) are unchanged* — and record the correction as ADR 0003 Amendment 1 (sub-decision 11), or as a numbered entry in the story's § Deviations, the way story 2 recorded its Amendment 1. No behavior change and no test change is needed: the code is right and the sentence is not.

### Harness friction
1. **A universal claim in an ADR reached the BIBLE and the API contract unverified.** ADR 0003 sub-decision 11 wrote "nothing else outside the generic signer's owner-only path" without a command showing it; the Architecture gate approved the sentence; the Tester's S-class pinned the route's registration and documentation but not the sentence; the Implementer copied it into BIBLE (twice), OpenAPI and two comments; and it fell to Review to run the one grep that disproves it (Blocking 1). The memory rule "Verify universal & ordering claims" exists for the human; the harness has no such step. Candidate `meta` row: an ADR sentence with "the only", "nothing else" or "the one route" carries the command that shows it (as the ADR's "Codebase facts" section already does for its file:line facts), and the review checklist's ADR-adherence row asks whether each such sentence was re-run.

Ledger `2026-09-22-gate-recipe-relative-require` (OPEN) covers the recipe's `require` path; this is the third story in a row to re-derive the rest. Candidate: the plan carries the executable recipe in full, or a committed helper under `test/helpers/` takes a pattern and drops `excluded` itself.
