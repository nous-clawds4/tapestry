### Non-blocking

1. **src/api/normalize/index.js (reconcile handler, schema-key resolution)** — ADR d4 says `<key>` is "the schema's sole top-level **required** key"; the implementation resolves the sole top-level **properties** key (refusing loudly when there isn't exactly one). Equivalent on every real wrapper (the convention keeps them identical — verified live on both concepts), equally loud on ambiguity, and it matches `extractSchemaObject` on the check side, so the two surfaces agree — arguably the better property. But it's an unlogged micro-departure from the ADR's letter. Optional: note it when story 3's ADR revisits this surface (Option C).
2. **src/api/normalize/index.js (reconcile read-compare-write)** — no lock: concurrent reconciles converge (identical derivation from the same schema), but a reconcile racing a concurrent `save-schema` could write a section derived from the pre-update schema. Bounded: operator-traffic surface, idempotent re-run converges, and the hygiene check catches any residue on its next run. Acceptable as shipped; Option C (folding reconciliation into `save-schema`, story 3's ADR) eliminates the race — worth carrying as a point in that decision.
3. **src/lib/brain/hygiene.js:143-146** — a missing/unreadable *schema* reports under kind `property-record-drift`; strictly it's schema-side unreadability, not record drift. The detail sentence names the real condition, so AC 4's specificity holds. Story 3's taxonomy extension may want a distinct kind rather than overloading this one.
4. **src/lib/brain/hygiene.js:92-96** — an incoming `HAS_ELEMENT` from a non-`Superset`-labeled node is neither counted as the class membership nor flagged — silently ignored. Correct per the ADR's taxonomy as written; noted so a future taxonomy extension makes the choice deliberately.
5. **src/api/normalize/index.js (reconcile `title` derivation)** — `(current && current.title) || schemaObject.title || key` preserves the existing record's title rather than strictly re-deriving per the create-concept generator. Cosmetic (the comparison ignores title), harmless, unlogged latitude.
