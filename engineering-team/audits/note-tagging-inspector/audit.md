## 1. What shipped

One capability, on every note surface at once: **the signed events behind a note's tag chips are now directly inspectable in-product.**

- A **"Show Raw Tagging Events" / "Hide Raw Tagging Events"** button in every note tag-chip's hover popover (beside Apply/Dispute, enabled signed in or out) toggles a per-(note, tag) panel inside the note's card — below the note's body, above the chips row — rendering **every** signed assertion the chip's counts are derived from — `stories/tag-event-inspector/3-note-tagging-raw-events-inspector.md`.
- Blocks are captioned by polarity + author pubkey, applications before disputes, uncounted viewer-own blocks marked; each block is the complete 7-field signed event, byte-faithful (the final gate-judge recomputed sha256 over the displayed event's canonical serialization and matched its `id`). Count the blocks, get the popover's numbers back.
- The pattern's shared core was extracted and shipped in the same story (ADR 0003 D5): `TagRowRawEvents` → the shared **`RawTaggingEvents`** blocks renderer (a rename — markup/classes byte-identical), plus the `toRawEvent` "as signed" projection now exported across both inspection APIs.

This is the **third inspection surface** of the epic's pattern (tag definition → profile taggings → note taggings) — the third and last tag-family object type (`nostr-event-tag`). The `_intake.md` `<RawEventPanel>` trigger ("extract at the third surface") fired and was discharged by D5.
