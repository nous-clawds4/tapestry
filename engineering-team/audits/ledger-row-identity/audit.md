## 1. What shipped

- **A new ledger row is a file with a date+slug id, minted without asking anyone.** `ledger/<YYYY-MM-DD-slug>.md`, copied from a template. Nothing is fetched and nothing is counted, so two sessions that cannot see each other mint different ids, and two new files never conflict at a merge. — `stories/done/ledger-row-identity/1-collision-free-ledger-row-ids.md`
- **The numbered table in `OPEN.md` is frozen where it stands, at row 343.** Every existing number finds the row it always found, and no citation anywhere was edited. — same story.
- **The last duplicate number was settled without a renumber.** Two rows were numbered 329. The one that landed first keeps the number; the later one moved, text unchanged, to `ledger/2026-09-19-agent-worktrees-outlive-books.md`, with a note where it stood. — same story.
- **`harness-lint` L15 holds the rule, and CI lints the merge result, so it is a merge gate.** An id twice in the table, a numbered row above the freeze, a date+slug id pasted into the table, and a malformed row file each fail by name. — same story.
- **The read surfaces treat both homes as one inbox.** `/whats-open`'s ledger section and "Meta items" list, the session-start digest and the meta escalation banner read row files alongside table rows. — same story.
- **One written rule; the interim rule is gone.** `OPEN.md` § "How to use this ledger" says how to mint, cite and close a row and what to do when two branches mint the same id. "Fetch first, expect to renumber" is retired from the book-close workflow and its command. Rows 151, 207 and 307 are DONE and the 2026-07-28 intake proposal is marked RESOLVED. — same story.
- **Product roles can file rows under the new rule.** The six writing product agents may write `ledger/**`. — same story.
- **The meta reader finds a row's Status by its value, not by counting pipes.** Open meta rows 70 and 244, invisible until then, are counted, listed and aged; the digest's count rose from 107 to 109 on purpose. — `stories/done/ledger-row-identity/2-meta-count-pipes.md`
- **Ledger bookkeeping along the way:** row 290 narrowed to its remaining defect (the 150-byte trim); row 330 filed in the design session and closed hours later as a duplicate of row 290 (PR #690); rows 331 and 332 filed by story #2's review; three rows filed as files by story #1's review.

While the book was in flight the old rule produced three more collisions: the two rows numbered 329 (found while the ADR was being written), and two renumbering merges later on 2026-09-19 (`8ccd69aa`, `37284673`) — the first of them because this book's own PR #691 took rows 331–332 ahead of a sibling's.
