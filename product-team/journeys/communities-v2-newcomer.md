# Journey: The Newcomer (Phase 2) — the cold-start foothold

**Slug:** communities-v2-newcomer
**Persona:** `product-team/personas/communities-v2-newcomer.md`
**Date:** 2026-06-06
**Builds on:** `journeys/communities-newcomer.md` (V1). The new material is steps 3–4: the true outsider's first foothold.

## Steps

### 1. First encounter (no account)
- **Trigger:** they are looking for a real community for an interest and are wary of fakes.
- **Action:** they land on Communities and browse circles without signing in.
- **Expected experience:** discovery works read-only. Each circle shows enough to judge it before any identity exists.
- **Emotional state:** curious, guarded.

### 2. Read whether the circle is alive (no account)
- **Trigger:** they have found a candidate circle.
- **Action:** they open it and look for signs of life and of who belongs.
- **Expected experience:** they can tell a living circle from a dormant one (recent activity, real conversation, faces) and can read the house trust signal for who actually belongs, all without an account. Impersonators visibly carry no weight.
- **Emotional state:** either "this place is real and active" or "this looks empty, move on." The first read is decisive.

### 3. Hit the trust wall (the cold-start moment)
- **Trigger:** they want in, but they trust no one in the graph and no one trusts them.
- **Action:** they look for a way to belong and find that vouches are the gate and they have zero.
- **Expected experience:** the wall is presented as a path, not a dead end. The product names how a first foothold is earned rather than saying "you are trusted by no one."
- **Emotional state:** the make-or-break moment. Either a visible path forward, or the bounce.

### 4. Earn a first foothold
- **Trigger:** a founder or member extends them an entry that does not require pre-existing trust (the discovery brief biases toward an invite that carries a vouch).
- **Action:** they accept the foothold and create their portable identity. **[Account required to belong.]**
- **Expected experience:** they enter legitimately through a person's extended trust, not an admin's approval. Their standing is real but starting, and the path from here to full belonging is visible.
- **Emotional state:** welcomed and accountable. They got in the honest way.

### 5. Cross over into belonging
- **Trigger:** they participate and trusted members vouch for them.
- **Action:** they introduce themselves, contribute, and accumulate vouches.
- **Expected experience:** their standing deepens visibly; they become a Belonger.
- **Emotional state:** they belong, and they can tell.

## Friction points
- **Dormant-looking circles (step 2):** if a living circle and an abandoned one look the same to a signed-out visitor, real circles lose newcomers they would have kept. Signs of life must be legible read-only.
- **Cold-start dead end (steps 3–4):** the central risk of the whole persona. If the outsider's only message is "you are trusted by no one," they leave and the model fails for exactly the user it most needs to serve.
- **Foothold becomes an admin gate (step 4):** if the foothold mechanism collapses into "wait for someone to approve you," it is the old closed door renamed and betrays the model.
- **Account-required cliff (step 4):** value must hold read-only as far as possible; the identity step should land only when belonging genuinely requires it, with typed or intended state surviving it.
