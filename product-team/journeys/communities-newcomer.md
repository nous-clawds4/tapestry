# Journey: The Newcomer

**Slug:** communities-newcomer
**Persona:** `product-team/personas/communities-newcomer.md`
**Date:** 2026-06-05

## Steps

### 1. First visit (no account) — load-bearing
- **Trigger:** searching for a circle around an interest.
- **Action:** discovers circles and opens one, without signing in.
- **Expected experience:** sees who belongs and — crucially — *how many people they already trust are inside*. The trust signal is legible **before any identity exists**.
- **Emotional state:** evaluating, cautious.

### 2. Assess trust
- **Trigger:** "is this the real circle, or a trap?"
- **Action:** scans the members and the trust around them.
- **Expected experience:** real/known members are legible; impersonators visibly carry no weight (no trusted person vouches for them).
- **Emotional state:** reassured (this is real) or warned (this is hollow) — either way, informed.

### 3. Decide to join
- **Trigger:** decides this is the real circle for them.
- **Action:** signs in and introduces themselves / asks to belong. **[Account required to act.]**
- **Expected experience:** a clear path — "introduce yourself and earn vouches," not "wait for an admin's approval."
- **Emotional state:** hopeful.

### 4. Earn belonging
- **Trigger:** members get to know them.
- **Action:** trusted members vouch; they cross the belonging threshold.
- **Expected experience:** a visible progression from "interested / applicant" to "member," driven by trust.
- **Emotional state:** accomplished — they belong, and it meant something.

### 5. Become a Belonger
- **Trigger:** they're in.
- **Action:** start participating and vouching. *(Hands off to the Belonger loop.)*
- **Expected experience:** seamless transition into full membership behavior.
- **Emotional state:** at home.

## Friction points
- **Cold-start (the biggest risk):** a genuine newcomer with *no* existing trust connections may never earn a first vouch. The product needs an answer for "how does an outsider get their first foothold" — carried forward as an open question.
- **Pre-identity trust signal:** if the trust signal is invisible until you sign in, the entire first-visit value proposition (tell real from fake) collapses. Read-only legibility is non-negotiable.
- **Limbo:** an "applicant" who can't tell whether they're progressing toward membership will give up.
