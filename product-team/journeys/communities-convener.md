# Journey: The Convener

**Slug:** communities-convener
**Persona:** `product-team/personas/communities-convener.md`
**Date:** 2026-06-05

## Steps

### 1. First encounter (no account)
- **Trigger:** frustrated that their group is owned by a platform/admin who could pull the rug.
- **Action:** lands on Communities and browses existing circles without signing in.
- **Expected experience:** sees circles that have no owner — membership shown as "people vouch for people," not "an admin let them in." The model is legible read-only.
- **Emotional state:** intrigued but skeptical ("how can a community have no admin?").

### 2. Decide to convene
- **Trigger:** wants their own circle on their terms.
- **Action:** signs in with a portable identity, then *declares* the circle — what it's about and what it takes to belong. **[Account required to found.]**
- **Expected experience:** guided to describe a circle and its belonging-bar, not to configure an admin control panel. It feels like convening people, not authoring a config object.
- **Emotional state:** relief — they're starting a community without signing up to be its dictator.

### 3. Seed it
- **Trigger:** a circle with no people isn't real yet.
- **Action:** vouches for a few people they already trust to seed membership.
- **Expected experience:** the circle becomes "real" as trusted people are vouched in — no approval queue; vouches do the work.
- **Emotional state:** empowered, a little exposed (will others come?).

### 4. Tend as a peer
- **Trigger:** newcomers want in; norms need light tending.
- **Action:** participates and vouches for people they trust, like any member.
- **Expected experience:** membership runs itself — trusted members' vouches admit newcomers; the convener is not a bottleneck.
- **Emotional state:** lighter — the weight of sole gatekeeping is gone.

### 5. Fork or step back
- **Trigger:** the circle outgrows them, or they disagree with where it's heading.
- **Action:** forks the circle's definition (carrying forward the people who agree) — or simply steps back.
- **Expected experience:** continuity without ownership; the circle survives a fork or their departure.
- **Emotional state:** free — proof the circle was never a hostage to them.

## Friction points
- **Founding mental model:** if "define the circle / set the belonging-bar" feels like authoring a protocol object rather than "start a circle," conveners bounce. Must be plain-language.
- **Seed cold-start:** if the people they vouch in must themselves already have identities/accounts, seeding stalls at zero. Flag.
- **Fork comprehension:** "fork a community" is an unfamiliar idea to non-technical conveners; the value (split without losing your people) must be obvious, the mechanic invisible.
