# Journey: The Belonger (Phase 2) — belonging that is felt, not just true

**Slug:** communities-v2-belonger
**Persona:** `product-team/personas/communities-v2-belonger.md`
**Date:** 2026-06-06
**Builds on:** `journeys/communities-belonger.md` (V1). The new material is making belonging perceptible in the room, on sovereign terms.

## Steps

### 1. Arrive inside
- **Trigger:** they have crossed from Newcomer to member; they belong.
- **Action:** they open the circle as a member for the first time.
- **Expected experience:** the room reads as a place with people and activity, not a static page. They can see who else is here and what has been happening.
- **Emotional state:** "I am actually part of this," not just "I am on a list."

### 2. Participate
- **Trigger:** there is a conversation, or they have something to say.
- **Action:** they post, reply, and react.
- **Expected experience:** conversation has texture: replies thread, reactions register, the room updates. Their contribution is visibly part of the circle.
- **Emotional state:** engaged. The circle responds to them.

### 3. Learn that something involving them happened
- **Trigger:** someone vouches for them, or replies to them, or the circle has new activity.
- **Action:** they find out, on terms they set.
- **Expected experience:** they are reached only in ways they pulled and configured. Being vouched for is something they get to feel. Nothing is scheduled at them to drag them back; they own their attention, their data, and their exit.
- **Emotional state:** seen and respected. The product is working for them, not on them.

### 4. Standing deepens
- **Trigger:** trusted people vouch for them over time.
- **Action:** they keep contributing.
- **Expected experience:** their belonging becomes legible and durable: they can tell they are trusted, and their voice carries more weight in who else belongs.
- **Emotional state:** rooted.

### 5. Vouch for others
- **Trigger:** a newcomer they trust wants in.
- **Action:** they vouch.
- **Expected experience:** their vouch visibly matters; it helps admit a real person and shape the circle. They are now part of the governance.
- **Emotional state:** consequential. Their trust does real work.

## Friction points
- **Correct but dead (step 1):** if membership is true in the data but the room is silent, the Belonger disengages and the system's engine stalls. Felt aliveness is the requirement, not a nicety.
- **Extraction creep (step 3):** the moment a notification is sent that the user did not ask for, cannot configure, or cannot turn off, the product has become the thing it rejects. The sovereignty test gates every reach-out.
- **Inconsequential vouch (step 5):** if vouching does not visibly matter, the governance loop dies and membership stops being emergent.
- **Non-portable belonging (steps 4–5):** if standing evaporates when they switch apps or the founder leaves, the durability promise breaks.
