# Journey: The Belonger

**Slug:** communities-belonger
**Persona:** `product-team/personas/communities-belonger.md`
**Date:** 2026-06-05

## Steps

### 1. First encounter (no account)
- **Trigger:** hears the circle they're already in elsewhere now exists here.
- **Action:** browses the circle without signing in; recognizes people.
- **Expected experience:** sees the circle's members and the trust between them, read-only.
- **Emotional state:** curious, reassured by familiar faces.

### 2. Join / get vouched
- **Trigger:** wants their standing in this circle to be real and theirs.
- **Action:** signs in, asserts they belong; trusted members vouch for them. **[Account required to belong or vouch.]**
- **Expected experience:** their standing appears because trusted people vouched — not because an admin flipped a switch. Belonging is legible ("you're in, and these trusted people vouch for you").
- **Emotional state:** welcomed, legitimate.

### 3. Participate
- **Trigger:** they have something to share.
- **Action:** posts and shows up in the circle's conversation.
- **Expected experience:** contributions are scoped to the circle and visible to members.
- **Emotional state:** at home.

### 4. Vouch for others
- **Trigger:** a newcomer they trust wants in.
- **Action:** vouches for them.
- **Expected experience:** their vouch carries weight proportional to their own standing; an untrusted person's dispute against the newcomer carries little. This is how they shape who belongs.
- **Emotional state:** invested, responsible — their judgment matters.

### 5. Portable, durable belonging
- **Trigger:** they move to another app/surface, or the founder leaves.
- **Action:** carry their identity and standing with them; keep belonging.
- **Expected experience:** belonging persists across surfaces and survives the founder's exit or a platform's death.
- **Emotional state:** secure — what they built is theirs.

## Friction points
- **Weightless-dispute legibility:** if a bad actor's dispute *appears* to threaten their standing equally, trust in the model erodes. Untrusted disputes must visibly carry ~no weight.
- **Belonging opacity:** "am I actually in? how trusted am I?" must be answerable at a glance, or the Belonger feels in limbo.
- **Vouch consequence:** if vouching feels like a meaningless "like," the engine of the system loses its fuel.
