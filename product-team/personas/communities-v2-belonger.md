# Persona: The Belonger (Phase 2)

**Slug:** communities-v2-belonger
**Priority:** Secondary
**Date:** 2026-06-06
**Builds on:** `personas/communities-belonger.md` (V1)

## Who they are
An active member who shows up, contributes, and vouches for others. V1 made their belonging *true* in the trust graph. Phase 2 is about making it *felt* in the room. They define themselves partly by the circles they are part of and the people who trust them, and they care that belonging is durable and theirs. The new gap they feel: their membership is real in the data but invisible in the experience. They cannot reply, react, see who else is here, or learn that someone vouched for them. Belonging that you cannot perceive does not feel like belonging.

## What they want
To belong durably among people they trust, and to experience that belonging as a living, legible thing in the circle, not only as a true fact in a graph.

## Their core loop
Participate in the circle (reply, react, converse) → learn when something involving them happens, such as being vouched for, on terms they control → see who else is here and what the circle is doing → their standing deepens as trusted people vouch for them → they vouch for newcomers, shaping who belongs.

## What they won't tolerate
- Belonging that is real in the data but dead in the experience: a correct roster in a silent room.
- Being pulled back by attention-capture machinery. A notification they did not ask for, cannot configure, or cannot turn off is the owned-platform pattern, and it violates the sovereignty the whole product promised them.
- Their vouch being meaningless, or a bad actor's dispute carrying the same weight as a trusted member's vouch.
- Losing the group, or their standing, if the founder leaves, the platform dies, or they switch apps.

## Notes
The Belonger's core loop is still the engine of the system, but Phase 2 reframes the risk. In V1 the risk was that vouching might not feel consequential. In Phase 2 the risk is that belonging might not feel *alive*, and that the obvious fixes (notifications, feeds, reactions) reach for the extraction patterns this product exists to reject. Every aliveness mechanism this persona touches must pass the sovereignty test from the discovery brief: the user owns their attention, their data, and their exit.
