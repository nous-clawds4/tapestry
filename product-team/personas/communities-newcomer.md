# Persona: The Newcomer

**Slug:** communities-newcomer
**Priority:** Secondary
**Date:** 2026-06-05

## Who they are
Someone evaluating whether and how to join a circle. Wary of scams and impersonators; today they rely on weak signals — follower counts, checkmarks, gut feel — and get burned. They want to find the *real* circle for their interest and get in the honest way, without either an opaque admin-approval limbo or a trust-free free-for-all.

## What they want
To find the real circle for their interest, tell the real members from impersonators, and join legitimately.

## Their core loop
Discover circles → assess who's actually inside and whether they trust those people (impersonators visibly carry no weight) → join by introducing themselves and earning vouches → cross over into being a Belonger.

## What they won't tolerate
- Not being able to tell the real circle/members from fakes.
- A join process that's either a closed door (waiting on an admin) or meaningless (anyone's "in," so being "in" means nothing).
- Investing effort and then getting ghosted with no visible path from "interested" to "belongs."

## Notes
The Newcomer → Belonger crossover is the critical conversion of the whole product. Two hard constraints fall out: (1) the **first visit must work read-only** — a brand-new person must be able to browse circles and read the trust signal *before any identity exists*; (2) the **cold-start problem** — how does a genuine newcomer earn their *first* vouch? — is the single biggest risk to this persona and is carried forward as an open question.
