# Persona: The Convener

**Slug:** communities-convener
**Priority:** Primary
**Date:** 2026-06-05

## Who they are
The person who gathers people. They start circles around an interest or a set of values and feel responsible for the people inside. Comfortable online; not necessarily technical. Defined by a tension: they want to *steward* a community without *ruling* it — they're allergic to being the single point of control and the single point of failure. They've run a Discord/group/subreddit before and felt the weight of unaccountable power and platform dependence.

## What they want
To bring the right people together in a space that can outlive them and can't be rug-pulled — including by themselves.

## Their core loop
Define what the circle is (its purpose and what it takes to belong) → seed it with a few people they trust → welcome newcomers by vouching, not by approving a queue → tend the norms as a peer → when there's growth or disagreement, fork the circle or step back, never having to own it as a dictator.

## What they won't tolerate
- Becoming an unaccountable admin doing manual gatekeeping forever.
- A platform (or a successor admin) that can delete, rename, or seize the circle.
- Being unable to leave or split without losing everyone they gathered.
- Impersonators diluting the circle with no way to keep the real members legible.

## Notes
The "dissenter/forker" from Discovery is this persona in a specific moment: founding and forking are the same muscle — branching a definition and carrying forward the people who agree. The product must make "start a circle" feel like convening people, not authoring a configuration object. The Convener's success condition is *being able to walk away* without the circle collapsing.
