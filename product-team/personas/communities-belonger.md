# Persona: The Belonger

**Slug:** communities-belonger
**Priority:** Primary
**Date:** 2026-06-05

## Who they are
An active member who shows up, contributes, and vouches for others. They define themselves partly by the circles they're part of and the people who trust them. They care about belonging that is *durable* and *theirs* — not a status an admin can revoke and not something that evaporates when they switch apps. They are, collectively, the governance: membership is the aggregate of belongers vouching for each other.

## What they want
To belong durably among people they trust, and to have that belonging be portable and their own — not revocable on one person's whim.

## Their core loop
Participate in the circle → vouch for newcomers they trust → their own standing deepens as trusted people vouch for them → they belong more fully, and their voice carries more weight in who else belongs. Discovery → action (participate/vouch) → reward (deeper standing + a shaped circle) → improved discovery (more trusted peers).

## What they won't tolerate
- Being removable without appeal by a single authority.
- Losing the whole group if the founder leaves or the platform dies.
- Standing that disappears when they move to a different app or surface.
- Their vouch being meaningless — or, worse, a bad actor's dispute carrying the same weight as a trusted member's vouch.

## Notes
The Belonger's core loop is the engine of the entire system — membership and governance emerge from it. If vouching doesn't feel consequential (my vouch visibly matters) and belonging doesn't feel legible (I can tell I'm in and trusted), this persona disengages and the whole model stalls.
