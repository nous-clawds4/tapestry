# Persona: The Convener (Phase 2)

**Slug:** communities-v2-convener
**Priority:** Primary
**Date:** 2026-06-06
**Builds on:** `personas/communities-convener.md` (V1)

## Who they are
The person who gathers people. They have already done the brave part: declared a circle around an interest or a set of values. Now they are standing in an empty room they made, and the room is silent. They are comfortable online and not necessarily technical. They are defined by the same tension V1 named, sharpened by the new moment: they want to make the circle *live* without becoming its owner. They fled the platforms where making a community active meant becoming its admin, and they will not accept a deal that hands that power back to them in a new wrapper.

## What they want
To grow a quiet circle into a living one that can outlive them and cannot be rug-pulled, including by themselves.

## Their core loop
Declare the circle → seed it with a few people they already trust → bring in people beyond their own reach (a newcomer who trusts no one yet can still get a first foothold through them) → generate the first signs of life so the room reads as alive → watch their own head start fade as the trust graph fills in and the circle starts running itself → tend as a peer, with caretaking acts (retiring, handling a bad actor) carried by the community's trust rather than a founder button → fork or step back, the circle surviving either.

## What they won't tolerate
- A deal that keeps them permanently special. Permanent founder power is the owner trap they came here to escape; if the head start never decays, the product lied to them.
- The opposite failure: being dropped at zero with no head start at all, so the cold-start wall makes the circle impossible to grow.
- Making the room feel alive only by importing attention-capture machinery (notifications that pull rather than serve) that violates the members' sovereignty.
- A circle that stays a quiet artifact: founded, correct, and dead, with no honest lever to grow it.
- Being unable to retire or clean up a circle they started, with no way to do it that does not require seizing owner power.

## Notes
The Phase 2 Convener is the V1 Convener one step later in time, at the moment the empty room becomes the central problem. Their success condition is unchanged and load-bearing: *being able to walk away* without the circle collapsing. The head-start-that-decays is the mechanism that lets them be a powerful first-mover now and a dispensable peer later; if a user cannot perceive that their special standing is fading on a legible rule, they will not trust that the product means what it says about no owners. Caretaking (the immediate need to retire three legacy test circles, and the larger moderation question for launch) is experienced by this persona as "how do I keep my circle healthy without becoming the cop."
